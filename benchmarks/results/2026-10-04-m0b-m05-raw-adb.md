**GO.** The S26 Ultra was captured over authorized USB ADB from X-pc.

The QNN library glob was not a sufficient compatibility test; NPU runtime compatibility remains M6 work.

## Important result

Raw USB ADB itself is fast enough for a development/control transport.

**UIAutomator is not fast enough for the main interactive perception loop.** Its tree dump is consistently about 2.07 s on this phone. It remains useful as a fallback/debug baseline only.

This makes direct Accessibility-service tree extraction (Mobilerun/A11yPilot-style) the next critical experiment.

## Verified control round-trip

1. Opened Samsung Settings.
2. Captured XML hierarchy and screenshot.
3. Parsed the visible `Connections` target from the hierarchy.
4. Resolved its clickable parent bounds.
5. Tapped the target (94.9 ms command round-trip).
6. Captured a new hierarchy.
7. Verified `Connections`, `Wi-Fi`, and `Bluetooth` were visible.

Classification: **PASS_VERIFIED**.

The phone screenshot was also successfully transported through X-pc/RDC and visually inspectable by the agent.
